Diploma in Hotel & Hospitality Management

Practical. Industry-Ready. Job-Focused.

Duration 1-Year / 2-Year
Eligibility Class 10 (any board)
Study Mode Full-Time
Start Sooner After Class 10

The Diploma in Hotel & Hospitality Management is a professionally designed program aimed at equipping students with the knowledge, skills, and practical exposure required for successful careers in the hospitality, tourism, and service industries. The curriculum integrates classroom learning with hands-on training to prepare students for employment in hotels, restaurants, resorts, airlines, cruise ships, and various hospitality-related organizations.

As one of the world's fastest-growing employment sectors, the hospitality industry offers numerous career opportunities both in India and internationally. This diploma program emphasizes service excellence, professional communication, personality enhancement, operational competencies, and industry-focused practical training to develop confident and competent hospitality professionals.

Program Goals

✓ Enhance industry-specific skills essential for hospitality careers
✓ Strengthen communication, leadership, and guest-service abilities
✓ Provide operational knowledge of hotel departments and hospitality services
✓ Develop practical competencies in food production, housekeeping, and front office
✓ Prepare students for internships, industry exposure, and professional employment

Food Production & Culinary Arts

  • Fundamentals of Food Production
  • Bakery and Confectionery Techniques
  • Kitchen Organization, Safety & Hygiene
  • Food Styling and Presentation

Food & Beverage Service

  • Restaurant Service Procedures
  • Beverage Operations and Management
  • Preparation of Mocktails and Cocktails
  • Banquet and Catering Management
03

Front Office Operations

  • Reception and Guest Service Management
  • Reservation Procedures and Systems
  • Guest Relations and Customer Care
  • Business Communication & Telephone Etiquette
04

Housekeeping Management

  • Housekeeping Functions and Practices
  • Laundry and Linen Management
  • Interior Decoration & Floral Arrangements
  • Cleanliness, Hygiene, and Sanitation Standards
05

Hospitality Management & Professional Skills

  • Introduction to the Hospitality Industry
  • Personality Development & Professional Grooming
  • English Communication and Soft Skills
  • Leadership and Teamwork
  • Computer Applications and Hospitality Software
06

Practical Learning & Industry Exposure

  • Industrial Training and Internship Programs
  • Practical Sessions in Training Kitchens & Restaurants
  • Educational Visits to Hotels & Hospitality Establishments
  • Guest Lectures by Industry Professionals
  • Food Festivals, Competitions & Hospitality Events
